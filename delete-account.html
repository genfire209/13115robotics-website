Delete Your Account

13115 Task Tracker · Last updated 2026-10-31

13115 Task Tracker is the internal app for FIRST Tech Challenge Team 13115 (Red Braves). If you've signed in to the app and want your account and data removed, here's how.

How to request deletion

  1. Email bravesred13115@gmail.com from the same Google or Apple email address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my account."
  2. We'll confirm your identity against that email address and remove your account within a few days.

There's no in-app "delete account" button yet — this is a small team app run by a student volunteer, and deletion requests go through a real person. We'll still honor every request.

What gets deleted

  • Your name and the email address tied to your account
  • Your team role and subteam assignment
  • Your device's push notification token
  • Your sign-in history

What may be kept

  • Tasks you created or were assigned may remain in the team's shared records with your name removed, since they're part of the team's task history, not personal data about you alone.

Contact

Questions about this process? Email bravesred13115@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
