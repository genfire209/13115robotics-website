Privacy Policy

13115 Task Tracker · Last updated 2026-10-31

13115 Task Tracker is an internal app built by and for FIRST Tech Challenge Team 13115 (Red Braves) to track tasks, meets, and match data for our own team. This page explains what the app collects and why, in plain language.

Who this is for

This app is built for our team's own members, who range in age from roughly 12 to 18. It is not intended for the general public. Sign-in is required, and a captain or admin has to approve each new member before they can use it.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address, from Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple — however you choose to sign in.
  • Your role on the team: subteam(s), whether you're a captain/admin, and whether you've been approved to use the app.
  • Task data you or teammates create: task titles, descriptions, due dates, who created or is assigned a task, its status, and any notes written on due-date extension requests.
  • A push notification token for your device, only if you allow notifications — used to tell you about tasks assigned to you or due soon.
  • Sign-in timestamps, used only to stop abuse (e.g. automated sign-in attempts), not to track you.

We don't show ads, don't use any analytics or ad-tracking tools, don't access your location, camera, or microphone, and don't sell or share your data with anyone outside running the app itself.

Who can see it

Other approved members can see your name, subteam, and the tasks assigned to or created by you — not your email address. Captains and admins can see real email addresses, since they're responsible for approving and managing the roster.

Services we use

  • Google and Apple, for sign-in.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), to deliver push notifications.
  • Microsoft Azure, to host the app's server and database.
  • FIRST's FTC Events API and FTCScout, to show public FTC team, match, and ranking information. This is public competition data about teams, not personal data about you.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you're an active member using the app. If you leave the team or want your data removed, email us (below) and we'll delete your account and associated data.

Parents and guardians

Since many of our members are minors, a parent, guardian, or team mentor can contact us at any time to review or request deletion of a student's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions, or want your data deleted? Email bravesred13115@gmail.com.
