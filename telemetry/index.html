TELEMETRY

The team app for FTC teams

Tasks, meet schedules with match alerts, scouting, a team calendar and announcements, and team tools: checklists, batteries, an engineering log, a parts inventory and a budget. Its assistant's AI runs on your own phone or computer, never on our servers.

Telemetry is an app for iPhone and Android, and works in a browser on your computer too.

Your captain registers your team, you join with the team's invite code, and everything stays inside your team. Telemetry is free, and we're testing it first with teams in New Jersey's Southern League.
