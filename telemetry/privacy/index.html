About Telemetry

Telemetry is a free app that helps FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) robotics teams run their team: tasks, meet schedules and match alerts, announcements, a calendar, scouting, and team tools like checklists and a parts inventory. It is made and run by students on FTC Team 13115, Brave Robotics, in New Jersey. "We" in this policy means the Telemetry team.

Telemetry is an app for iPhone and Android, and also works in a web browser on a computer at 13115robotics.com/telemetry/app/. This policy covers all of them.

Telemetry is not made by or affiliated with FIRST. Each team that uses Telemetry is approved by us, and its captain manages who joins it.

Who can use Telemetry

Telemetry is for members, mentors and coaches of FTC teams who are 13 or older. We ask you to confirm your age the first time you sign in.

You sign in with a Google account, or on iPhone with Sign in with Apple. On a computer, you sign in with Google. You can only use Telemetry as part of a team: you join one with an invite code from its captain, and the captain approves you.

Information we do not collect

Telemetry has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools of its own, and never sells or rents your information. We do not collect your location, contacts, payment details or advertising ID.

  • Camera: used to scan a team's invite QR code, and to take a photo when you add one to the engineering log. A QR scan takes no picture.
  • Photos: engineering log photos stay on your phone. They are never uploaded to Telemetry; we only learn how many photos an entry has, so your teammates know to ask you for them. The app shrinks each photo and removes its hidden details (EXIF), including where it was taken, before keeping it. Only a captain's team logo is uploaded. Deleting the app or your account deletes the log photos on that phone, so save copies you want to keep.

The assistant

Telemetry's assistant answers questions about your team using the AI built into your phone: Apple Intelligence on iPhone, or Gemini Nano on supported Android phones. The AI runs on the phone itself. Telemetry's servers never run AI on your questions.

  • The AI reads only team data the app has already shown you and saved on your phone (the tasks, inventory, checklists and other team information). That data isn't sent anywhere for the assistant.
  • Your chats are saved to your Telemetry account, so they show up on every phone you sign in on. We store the text of each chat (your questions, the answers, and the team items they link to) on our server, like the rest of your Telemetry information.
  • Only you can see your chats. Your teammates, captain and admins can't.
  • You keep up to 50 chats; past that, your oldest chat is deleted.
  • You can delete one chat or all of them in the assistant. That deletes them from your account and every device. Signing out removes them from that phone only; they stay on your account.
  • Your chats are deleted when you leave or are removed from your team, when your team is deleted, or when you delete your account. They talk about your team's information, so they don't follow you to another team.
  • On phones without on-device AI, the assistant only searches the team data on your phone.
  • On a computer, the AI runs on the computer, in your browser. The assistant uses the first of these that works: Chrome's built-in AI (Gemini Nano), which Google says runs on the device; Ollama, if you run it on your computer and turn it on in the assistant; or a small open AI model (Qwen2.5 1.5B) that your browser downloads once, about 1 GB, and keeps. That download comes from Hugging Face and GitHub; the software that runs it (WebLLM) comes from our own site. Your questions and the answers are never sent to any of them. If none of these work on your computer, the assistant only searches your team's data.
  • iPhone: the assistant uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models, which Apple says run on the phone. Telemetry doesn't send your chats to Apple.
  • Android: the assistant uses Gemini Nano through Google's ML Kit, which runs on the phone. Google's documentation says ML Kit sends Google limited diagnostic information: the phone's model and system version, which AI features were used and how long they took, and the language setting. Google's list of what it collects does not include your questions or the answers.

Telemetry on a computer

  • Your sign-in, and the team data the assistant answers from, are kept in your browser's storage for 13115robotics.com on that computer. Signing out clears them.
  • The web version has no notifications and no engineering log photos (photos stay on the phone that took them).
  • The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and Google's sign-in button from Google. Like any download, those services see your computer's internet address.

Public robotics data

Match schedules, scores, rankings, and team names, schools and cities come from FIRST's public FTC Events API. Team-name search and season statistics come from FTCScout, a public FTC statistics site. This is public information about teams, not about you.

When the app asks these services for data, it sends only team numbers and event codes, never anything about you.

How we use information

We use your information only to run Telemetry for you and your team:

  • show your team's tasks, announcements, calendar, meets and scouting;
  • send notifications, such as a new task, an approval, or "your match is in 10 minutes";
  • email you when you register a team, to say it was approved (with its invite code) or why it wasn't. We send no other email, and never marketing;
  • let captains and admins manage their team;
  • review new team registrations and reports, and stop misuse;
  • fix problems and keep the app running.

We never use it for advertising, and we never sell or share it for anyone else's marketing.

Where data is stored, and how it's protected

Your information is stored in the United States, on Supabase's servers in the eastern US.

  • Everything sent between the app and our server is encrypted.
  • Our server checks on every request that you belong to the team whose data you're asking for.
  • A backup of the database is made every night, encrypted, and kept for 30 days in a private GitHub repository. The key that unlocks the backups is never stored on GitHub.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we will tell affected teams and users promptly.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account and your team are active.

  • Deleting your account (Profile → Delete account): your name, email, sign-in ID and notification tokens are removed right away. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also tell Apple to disconnect Telemetry. Your assistant chats are deleted. Tasks and other things you made stay with your team, shown as by "Deleted user", so your team's history still makes sense. A captain must hand captaincy to someone else, or delete the team, before deleting their own account.
  • Leaving or being removed from a team: you lose access to that team's information, and your assistant chats are deleted. Your account stays until you delete it.
  • Deleting a team (captain only): the team's tasks, announcements, calendar, scouting, team tools, logo and everyone's assistant chats for that team are deleted. Engineering log photos were never on our servers; they stay on each person's phone until they delete them.
  • Backups: deleted information disappears from backups within 30 days, as old backups expire.

You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing telemetry.ftc13115@gmail.com.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your information: your name and subteams are on your Profile; your captain can fix your role.
  • Notifications: turn them off anytime in your phone's settings.
  • Get a copy, or have it deleted: delete your account in the app, or email telemetry.ftc13115@gmail.com for a copy or deletion. We reply within 30 days.
  • Report a problem: use Report in the app, or email us.

Parents and guardians can email telemetry.ftc13115@gmail.com to see, correct or delete their child's information.

Children's privacy

Telemetry is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. FTC allows some 12-year-olds on teams; they can join Telemetry once they turn 13.

If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we will delete it. Parents who think this has happened can email telemetry.ftc13115@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and post the new version at this address. If a change affects how your information is used, we will also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
