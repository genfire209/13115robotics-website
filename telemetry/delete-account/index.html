Delete your account

Telemetry

In the app

Open Profile, then Delete account. It happens right away.

If you're your team's captain, hand captaincy to a teammate first (Team tab), or delete the team.

Without the app

Email telemetry.ftc13115@gmail.com from the address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account. We'll do it, and reply, within 30 days.

What's deleted

  • Your name, email address, sign-in ID and notification tokens, right away.
  • Your assistant chats.
  • Engineering log photos only ever lived on your phone; deleting your account deletes them on that phone.
  • Backups of our database expire within 30 days, so your information is gone from them by then.

What stays

Tasks, log entries and other things you made for your team stay with the team, shown as by "Deleted user", so your team's history still makes sense. Your captain can delete them.

See the privacy policy for the details.
